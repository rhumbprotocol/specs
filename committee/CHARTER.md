# RWP™ Committee Charter

The Rhumb Workflow Protocol™ (RWP™) Committee provides stewardship and direction for the protocol specification, schemas, templates, and ecosystem.

---

## Purpose

The RWP™ Committee exists to:

- Guide the evolution of the protocol specification
- Review and approve significant changes to core artifacts
- Maintain the quality and consistency of the protocol ecosystem
- Foster an inclusive, collaborative contributor community
- Ensure backward compatibility and stability for adopters

---

## Principles

The committee operates under these guiding principles:

1. **Openness** - All discussions, decisions, and meeting notes are public
2. **Minimal governance** - Process should enable contribution, not impede it
3. **Technical merit** - Decisions are based on technical quality, not politics
4. **Backward compatibility** - Breaking changes require strong justification
5. **Advisory by nature** - RWP™ recommends; it does not enforce

---

## Structure

### Roles

| Role | Description | Count |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Chair** | Facilitates meetings, sets agenda, breaks ties | 1 |
| **Maintainer** | Reviews PRs, merges changes, manages releases | 2-5 |
| **Committer** | Write access to the repository, trusted contributor | 3-10 |
| **Contributor** | Anyone who submits issues, PRs, or discussions | Unlimited |

### Current Committee

The initial committee is appointed by the protocol creator ([YAKKL, Inc.](https://yakkl.com)):

- **Chair**: YAKKL, Inc. (founding chair)
- **Maintainers**: To be appointed as the community grows

As the community matures, committee membership will transition to a community-elected model.

---

## Membership

### Becoming a Committer

Contributors who demonstrate sustained, quality contributions may be nominated for committer status:

- Consistent contributions over a period of time (typically 3+ months)
- Demonstrated understanding of the protocol specification
- Constructive participation in reviews and discussions
- Nomination by an existing maintainer, approved by lazy consensus

### Becoming a Maintainer

Committers who take on broader responsibility may be promoted to maintainer:

- Active involvement in release management
- Track record of thoughtful PR reviews
- Demonstrated ability to make balanced technical decisions
- Nomination by the chair or another maintainer, approved by committee vote

### Stepping Down

Committee members may step down at any time by notifying the chair. Inactive members (no contributions for 6+ months) may be moved to emeritus status after a friendly check-in.

---

## Meetings

- **Frequency**: As needed, typically monthly or quarterly depending on activity
- **Format**: Asynchronous by default (GitHub issues/discussions); synchronous calls when needed
- **Notes**: Published in the repository under `committee/minutes/`
- **Quorum**: A majority of maintainers for formal decisions

---

## Amendments

This charter may be amended by a committee vote (simple majority of maintainers). Proposed amendments should be submitted as a pull request and discussed for a reasonable period (typically 2 weeks) before voting.
